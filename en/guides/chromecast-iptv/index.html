Chromecast and IPTV: how to cast video to a TV

Casting asks a compatible receiver to play media on another screen. It is not necessarily the same as mirroring your phone display.

Understanding the issue

With Google Cast, the phone often acts as a controller while the receiver plays the stream itself. That receiver must be able to access the stream and decode its format. A file playing locally in VLC or ExoPlayer therefore may not work over Cast.

Interruptions can involve the local network, sleep state, unsupported codecs, expired media URLs or content rights. Casting availability also depends on the sending app, receiver and provider rules.

Checklist and next steps

  1. Confirm phone and Cast receiver use networks that allow device discovery.
  2. First test an authorized video known to work on that receiver.
  3. Check that the stream format and codec work on the receiver, not just on the phone.
  4. If casting stops, test Wi-Fi stability, sleep behavior and whether the media URL expired.
  5. If the feature remains unreliable, try an Android TV player installed directly on the television.

Key takeaway

Playback working on a phone does not imply universal Chromecast compatibility.
