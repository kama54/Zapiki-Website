M3U vs Xtream Codes: what is the difference?

M3U and Xtream are different ways of supplying an IPTV player with the information needed to access your authorized streams.

Understanding the issue

An M3U playlist is usually a text file or URL listing media entries, often accompanied by channel names, groups and EPG identifiers. The list may be static or updated by its provider. The player resolves the stream references it receives.

An Xtream-style connection normally requires a server URL, username and password. Depending on provider and software, it can expose live, movie and series categories. Credentials do not grant broadcast rights or guarantee that every stream will work.

Checklist and next steps

  1. Ask your lawful source provider which connection format it supports.
  2. For M3U, copy the complete URL or select the local file without deleting query parameters.
  3. For Xtream, enter the exact server address, optional port, username and password.
  4. Refresh the source and check whether the expected channels and categories appear.
  5. Never publish signed M3U URLs, tokens or login details in screenshots or forum posts.
