Understanding the issue

A phone app does not automatically provide a good ten-foot interface. On a television, look for accessible focus indicators, readable text, remote-friendly navigation and clear categories.

Android TV and Fire TV models differ in software versions, app-store availability, permissions and playback capabilities. Installing a player does not include a content subscription or distribution rights.

Checklist and next steps

  1. Verify your television or streaming stick model, software version and app compatibility.
  2. Install the player from an official or otherwise trusted distributor.
  3. Sign into your account if needed and add an authorized M3U playlist or compatible Xtream access.
  4. Test Live TV, Movies, Series and Favorites using only the remote control.
  5. Confirm playback stability, available audio tracks and EPG before tweaking advanced settings.
